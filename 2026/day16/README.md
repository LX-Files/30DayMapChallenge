<h1>DAY 16 - COLLABORATIVE MAP</h1>
<h2>CROWDSOURCED EXACTITUDE IN GEOGRAPHY</h2>
<p>I do not have a collaborative map at hand. However, everybody can collaborate on a specific project, OpenStreetMap. As an addition to my Borgesian Map interpretation, the same area, exact in its geography, can be used as a prime example of a collaborative map. So, here it is, a rather squarish segment of geographic data centred on the city of Lisbon, showing 1.510.712 nodes, updated by 3171 different users. Their names are listed on the side column, which I leave here with a deep 'thank you'.</p>
<p>OSM data processed with command line tools. Raster editing in <b>Photoshop</b>. Final composition in <b>Illustrator</b>. Font used: <b>FiraSans</b>.<br>
Map data  © OpenStreetMap contributors.</p>
