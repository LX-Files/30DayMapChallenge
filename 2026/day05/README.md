<h1>DAY 05 - SENSES: SIGHT</h1>
<h2>ONE OF THE BEST PANORAMIC VIEWS OF LISBON THAT YOU CAN (NO LONGER) SEE</h2> 
<p>The red dot on the satellite image marks the location of the building from where this panoramic view could be seen. The building itself, located in the Forest Park of Monsanto, is shown on the right in its 2021 look.</p>
<p>The first ideas of building something in that location date from the 1930s. Actual planning and construction occurred during the 1960s. It was inaugurated in 1970 as a restaurant with an amazing view over Lisbon. However, it functioned only as such for two years. Until 2001 it was ocasionally used again as a restaurant, as a storehouse or as a nightclub. After being abandoned for almost two decades, it reopened in 2017 as a municipal viewpoint, still showing original works of art from its early beginning, such as ceramics, painting, tiles and sculptures, together with several modern pieces of urban art. It was once again closed, in 2023, due to safety concerns regarding the building.</p>
<p>I took these photos in 2021 using a mobile phone, with the intention of repeating them with better equipment. 
I missed that opportunity.</p>
<p>While the building offered a 270º field of view, the panorama presented here does not cover that full extent (only 230º), due both to the presence of vegetation and to some structural limitations. The satellite image provides the broader location of each point of interest identified. On a clearer day the full distance view would be amazing.</p>
